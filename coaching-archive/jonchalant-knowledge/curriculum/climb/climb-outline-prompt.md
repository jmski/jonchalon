## Context

### The strategy doc tells you the full picture, but the essentials:

**The Foundation** is a 3-stage / 6-month coached engagement. Three stages: **The Climb** (4-6 weeks, 7 lessons) · **The Vantage** (12-16 weeks, 10 lessons) · **The Leap** (6-18 months, 11 lessons; 8 effective per user).

**The Climb is the inventory stage.** Honest cataloguing of what's already in the student's life. Not discovery — *inventory*. The work is not finding hidden truths; it's being honest about what's there and what isn't. The student sorts what they do across the four ikigai circles (Passion, Mission, Vocation, Profession), identifies the one they've been avoiding, chooses a physical medium and starts practicing in it, and mourns what isn't working.

**Completion criteria for The Climb:** populated inventory, named probable missing circle, chosen physical medium and started practicing it, named what's being let go of.

### Brand voice non-negotiables

- **"As long as it takes"** is the timing message — never "transform in X weeks"
- **Banned words:** unlock, transform, journey, empowered, authentic self, limiting beliefs, inner game, holistic, level up, breakthrough, mindset shift, self-mastery, soul work
- **No urgency tactics**, no motivational language, no coaching-genre gloss
- **Embodiment is the moat** — the body practice IS the lesson, not an exercise appended to a concept. Concept articulated AFTER the body has felt it.
- **Two registers:** A (direct/honest, names hard things) + B (warm/present, sits with the reader). The Climb leans Register A — the work is uncomfortable; lying about that breaks the voice.

### Audience

Quietly misaligned corporate professionals. Well-paid, competent, in-demand. Missing one of the four ikigai circles (most often Mission or Passion). They've been performing their job competently while sensing it's not quite right. The Climb is the first time someone has asked them to inventory what's actually there.

### Medium-agnostic claim

Every Foundation student picks a physical medium. Jon's is dance. The student's can be hiking, climbing, weight training, partner dance, swimming, martial arts, yoga, or other practices that meet the criteria: requires presence, offers feedback, sustainable for years, not status-driven.

**Lesson 4 ("Finding your physical medium") is where the student picks theirs.** This means lessons 1-3 introduce embodied work *before* the student has committed to a specific medium. Their body practice in those early lessons is general (body scan, breath awareness, body-state tracking) — not yet medium-specific. From lesson 4 onward, the body practice IS in their chosen medium.

This is a structural constraint on the outline. Honor it.
