# Program & Brand Strategy

> Source-of-truth for Jonchalant. Drafted May 2026, evolved through several sessions.
> v1.0 (initial strategy) → v1.1 (stage names + kinetic line) → v1.2 (brand evolution, visual frame, content ops, launch sequencing) → **v1.3 (current — visual specifics, podcast format, organization system, founding cohort experience, codebase licensing).**

---

## Summary

Jonchalant is a coaching practice + media brand built around a single argument: **the body knows what the mind is still arguing about.** The work is finding your ikigai (which one of the four circles you've been missing) and learning to inhabit your work — through an embodied practice in a medium of your choosing.

Three public surfaces:

1. **The Foundation** — three-stage / six-month coached engagement (The Climb · The Vantage · The Leap), with standalone modules for Stages 1-2, an Ongoing subscription for graduates, and (Year 2+) a Coach Training Program.
2. **The podcast** — *Jonchalant and the Iki-Guys*, featuring guests whose work and lives align across decades.
3. **The Lab** — Jon's running self-documentation through the curriculum, short video + companion blog. Plus standalone essays and a Tuesday newsletter.

Five structural decisions underneath everything:

1. **Four-pillar framework retired entirely.** No teaching vocabulary, no card primitive, no branded concept.
2. **Embodiment integrated deeply.** Body practice IS the lesson; concept articulated after the body has felt it. The moat. Non-negotiable.
3. **Visual frame: Silk Sonic specifically.** Late-night soul, intimate scale, restrained craft. Recoleta + Fraunces. Mocha Mousse palette (already in `design-system`).
4. **Brand publicly includes the Iki-Guys from launch.** *Jonchalant and the Iki-Guys.* Iki-Guys are featured guests, not trained coaches.
5. **Brand is media-forward.** Content is its own product line, not just marketing.

## The three-stage structure

### Stage names (locked)

- **Stage 1 — The Climb** — 4-6 weeks, 7 lessons. Honest inventory.
- **Stage 2 — The Vantage** — 12-16 weeks, 10 lessons. Active integration.
- **Stage 3 — The Leap** — 6-18 months, 11 lessons (8 effective). Structural change. Coaching-essential.

**Origin (internal only):** Names arose from Assassin's Creed synchronization sequence. **AC reference never appears in public copy.**

Names were considered for renaming under the band-frame pivot (The Demo · The Session · The Drop, etc.) and rejected. The climb/vantage/leap names describe a *progression*; musical alternatives are *vibes*. Right place for band aesthetic is the *frame around* the work. Substance underneath, swagger on top.

### Timeline truth

*"Eight weeks. One week at a time."* is retired.

> The Climb takes 4-6 weeks. The Vantage takes 12-16 weeks. The Leap takes 6-18 months. **The Foundation is a 6-month coached engagement** that gets users through the Climb and the Vantage and into the Leap. Leap work continues beyond, supported by the Ongoing subscription.

*"As long as it takes"* is the brand voice expression.

## Visual frame — Silk Sonic specifically

### Typography (locked)

- **Display: Recoleta.** Hero moments, headlines, stage cards, podcast covers. Used in thick weights, often uppercase. Has the flair/personality Jon wanted (the *g*, *Q*, *R* leg).
- **Body: Fraunces.** Editorial moments, body copy, italic anchor convention.
- **Italic anchor convention** (`{{double-braces}}`) stays on Fraunces.

### Palette (locked)

**Mocha Mousse base** (already in `design-system`). Pairs with:
- Cream / ivory (warm neutral)
- Brass / antique gold (metallic accent)
- Oxblood (emphasis only, never dominant)
- Smoke gray / charcoal (B&W imagery, contrast)

Burnt indigo / muted moss retired.

### Photography direction (locked)

- Large landscape compositions with bolded shadowed text in the middle (film-poster energy)
- One-color schemes with B&W imagery; strategic saturated pops
- Low light, warm interiors, velvet textures, vintage microphones, single-spotlight portraits
- Mid-century Blue Note Records sensibility for guest portraits — high-contrast geometric portraiture as signature elements (not low-poly objects scattered as decoration)

### Visual moments / metaphor extension

- Three stage cards can take album-side / track listing treatments
- Foundation = album; Ongoing = b-sides; Lab = liner notes; podcast = guest features
- Tasteful, not forced

### Still open

- Final palette hex codes (verify in `design-system`)
- Logo (Toronto brand designer, ~$1,500-$4,000)
- Vinyl plaque design (same designer)
- Photography commission (12-18 months out)
- Favicon / app icon mark (abstract three-element; not literal mountain)

### Risks

- **Costume problem.** Funk/soul on a non-musician reads as performative. Adjacent to music, not pretending to be it.
- **Corporate audience recoil.** Warm and honest stays dominant; Silk Sonic frames it.
- **Aesthetic drift.** Silk Sonic specifically. Don't slide toward Bruno Mars pop or hip-hop visuals.

### "What if it works" — Scenario planning

- **Scenario A (likely):** modest steady growth. Stay at Life Time part-time.
- **Scenario B (strong):** drop Life Time to once-a-week for perks. Revenue $150-200K. The Jon-target version.
- **Scenario C (breakout):** demand exceeds capacity. **Locked plan: raise prices, run waitlist, don't dilute the offer.** Foundation moves to $7,500, $10,000, higher. Bring forward first trained coach. Hire VA/operations support ($1,500-3,000/mo) if hours exceed 30/week.

## The Iki-Guys (featured guests + podcast)

### Definition (locked)

**An Iki-Guy is someone whose work and life align — *visibly*, *honestly*, *across decades* — and who teaches what they know, or shares it generously.**

- **Visibly:** alignment in body of work, not in posts
- **Honestly:** can talk about cost, mourning, scars
- **Across decades:** time tested the alignment
- **Teaches/shares:** workshop runners, mentors, public-facing practitioners

### Why Iki-Guys are guests, not coaches

Originally considered as the long-term name for trained coaches. **Rejected.** Instead:
- **Iki-Guys = featured guests in the podcast.** Launchable day one.
- **Trained coaches (Year 2+) get a different designation.** Working placeholder: *Jonchalant Practitioners* or similar. Lock when program is real.
- **Overlap rare but allowed.** A trained practitioner who's also an Iki-Guy because they're a decade-deep practitioner in their original medium gets both designations.

### Network breadth

Jon's network: real estate, food, music, security, personal training, photography, content creation, and more. **Music is the deep cut** — working musicians are exactly the brand vibe. **Security is the surprise category** — people who've spent years reading rooms and bodies. **Food is natural** — chefs are body-workers who don't always realize they are.

**By month 6:** at least 3 Iki-Guys from mediums outside Jon's current network reach (writer, chef, craftsperson, builder, therapist, trades). Operational task.

### Candidate vetting

The triad is a self-check before reaching out, not a gatekeeping test for incomplete profiles. Get full context first.

## The podcast — *Jonchalant and the Iki-Guys*

### Format (locked)

- Audio-first, 45-75 min per episode
- Long-form conversation, no chopping
- Lightly produced — two people, two mics, real talk. Cold open from most striking moment.
- Companion blog post per episode
- Video optional per episode

### Interview architecture (locked)

**Anchor (every episode):** *"What were you doing the year before you knew this was your work?"*

**Deeper cut 1:** *"What did you almost do instead, and what stopped you?"*

**Deeper cut 2:** *"What do you do for a living that doesn't quite match what you're actually for?"*

Anchor always asked. Deeper cuts available when the conversation has room. Room for improv on top.

### Production cadence (locked)

**One episode every 4-6 weeks.** Real commitment, not when-we-have-time. Prep is 3-5 hours per episode. Total workload ~8-12 hours/episode including blog post.

### Launch-day content state (locked)

Site goes live with **3 episodes already in the feed.** Record 2-3 episodes pre-launch (Phase 11.5).

### Short-form companion: "Same Question, Many Voices"

New format added v1.3: same single question to 8-12 people from different industries, spliced into 60-90 second vertical video. Anchor question (or close variant) is the question.

Does brand work the long-form can't:
- Medium-agnostic claim visually compressed
- Natively shareable
- Feeds the podcast
- Content-efficient (one recording day, 8-10 short answers, edited into one piece)

### Production setup (Phase 11.5)

- Mic: Shure SM7B ($400) or Rode PodMic ($130)
- Interface: Focusrite Scarlett ($170)
- Editing: Descript ($20/mo)
- Hosting: Transistor, Buzzsprout, or Spotify for Podcasters

All-in: $600-800 equipment, $20/mo software.

## Founding cohort experience design

### Welcome moment

- **Welcome video** from Jon. 90 seconds, personal, not corporate.
- **Handwritten card** mailed to each cohort member. Their name. A single specific question to sit with as they start the Climb: *"As you start, hold this question: what would you have to mourn before you could change?"*

### Stage transition moments

- Climb → Vantage: dedicated check-in session as ritual. Jon names accomplishments, asks them to articulate what shifted.
- Vantage → Leap: same structure, different stakes.

### The Leap arrival

When client makes their structural change: direct acknowledgment in session. *"You did the thing. That doesn't happen often. I'm proud of you."* Said with weight. Worth more than any badge.

### Graduation

- Final session at month 6
- **Written reflection from Jon** — a paragraph specifically about this person's work, theirs to keep
- **Vinyl-record-style plaque** — LP for founding, single for subsequent. Hand-numbered. Their name in the dead wax.

### Per-cohort artifact budget

~$500-1000 in physical artifacts (handwritten cards, plaque, packaging, postage).

## Coach Training pathway (Year 2+)

### Philosophy (locked)

Help **exceptional** Foundation graduates become practitioners. The bar is high — ~1 in 10 graduates would be invited. Program respects the graduate's choice: **operate under Jonchalant umbrella OR start their own practice.**

### Default path: working under the umbrella

Becoming a Jonchalant practitioner (designation TBD). Takes clients under Jonchalant brand, receives Jon's referrals, revenue-share TBD. The accessible path.

### Alternative path: licensing the operational template

For exceptional cases where a practitioner wants a fully independent practice using Jon's operational infrastructure (codebase, Sanity setup, brand-deployment guidance):

- **Commercial transaction, not a gift.** Paid licensing, meaningful sum (TBD, likely $50K+).
- **Personally reviewed by Jon.** Not self-serve.
- **Intentionally hard.** Umbrella path is the obvious choice for most.
- **Attribution required.** Credit Jon as training source, Jonchalant as originating methodology.

### Long-term timeline

- Year 1: Foundation launches. Coach Training mapped conceptually.
- Year 2: Coach Training built. Top 1-2 graduates invited.
- Year 3: First trained practitioner begins taking clients.
- Year 4-5: 3-5 active. Jon's primary work shifts to training/supervising, brand and content.

### IP/licensing (deferred)

Specifics designed when program launches. Open licensing (with attribution) and method protection both viable.

Highest-value 20 min/week against drift.

## How to leverage Claude (locked patterns)

**What Claude is good at:**
- Holding full context across docs and decisions
- Pushing back on instincts that feel off (no social investment to flatter)
- Filling structural gaps in plans
- Reformulating bad questions into good ones
- Producing volume drafts on demand (for infrastructure, not voice content)

**What Claude is NOT good at:**
- Writing curriculum lessons in Jon's voice from scratch (you draft, I review)
- Remembering between conversations (always paste source docs)
- Producing fresh creative direction without your input
- Knowing when to stop generating ideas without being cued

**Workflows to use Claude for:**
- "Keep me honest" — bring a draft, check for banned-word drift, voice mismatch, contradiction
- "Fill the gap" — describe a half-formed plan, ask what's missing
- "Help me think through this" — for real decisions with long tails
- "Reformulate" — when intent is right but execution could be sharper
- "Audit and review" — periodic (4-6 weeks), check for staleness
- "Volume drafts" — for infrastructure (policies, boilerplate), not content

**Critical pattern:** always paste current strategy doc + canonical-copy as context in new chats. Don't trust my memory; trust my reasoning given current context.

## Brand voice non-negotiables

- **No coaching-genre language.** Banned: unlock, transform, journey, empowered, authentic self, limiting beliefs, inner game, holistic, alignment (personal-dev), level up, breakthrough, mindset shift, self-mastery, soul work.
- **No urgency tactics.** Founding cohort framing is the only urgency mechanic, stated as fact.
- ***"As long as it takes"*** is the timing message everywhere.
- **Premium pricing presented honestly.** Show list price. Don't apologize.
- **Embodiment is the moat.** Body practice IS the lesson, not an add-on.
- **Silk Sonic in the frame, not in the work.** Visual identity = Silk Sonic. Stage names, lessons, coaching = structural honesty.
- **Iki-Guys are guests, not coaches.** Name must not drift.
- **The dashboard pulls; nothing pushes.** Creative work is invited, not coerced.
